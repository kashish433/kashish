Laboratory Facilities:

The College has well-equipped and well-managed laboratories for all

the Science Courses offered viz. Bio-Chemistry, Botany, Chemistry, Physics, Bio-technology, Computer Science and Zoology. The Departments of Nutrition & Health Education, NANS, Functional English, Music and Home Science also have separate well equipped labs. The College has five well-equipped Computer Labs with Pentium machines with the latest configuration. The Labs have connectivity. There is a separate Computer lab facility in the Department of Bio-Chemistry. The Departments of Botany, Chemistry, Zoology and Mathematics have their own computers.
